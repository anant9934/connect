## 1. Search Strategy Overview & Core Principles

The Tathastu Meet organic search strategy prioritizes high-intent, capability-backed discovery over generic keyword dumping:

1. **Brand Ownership First:** Establish unimpeachable authority over brand variations ("Tathastu Meet", "Tathastu Meet app", "Tathastu Meet official website").
2. **Intent-to-Page Mapping:** Map each search cluster to a specific, verified URL that provides a genuine, helpful answer to that exact search intent.
3. **No Doorway Pages or Keyword Stuffing:** Zero empty pages, zero hidden keyword blocks, and zero fabricated claims. Google rankings are driven by content depth, performance, crawlability, and schema clarity.
4. **Competitor Alternative Honesty:** Target comparison searches ("Zoom alternative", "Google Meet alternative") through factual, transparent architectural comparisons without trademark infringement or deceptive affiliation claims.

## 3. SEO Non-Regression & Quality Assurance Rules

1. **No Keyword Stuffing:** Keywords are integrated organically into semantic content, technical explanations, and headings.
2. **Metadata Integrity:** Every public page has a unique `<title>` (using the `%s | Tathastu Meet` template), a human-readable `<meta name="description">`, and a clean canonical URL.
3. **Structured Data:** Valid JSON-LD schemas (`WebSite`, `Organization`, `SoftwareApplication`, `BreadcrumbList`, `FAQPage`) are verified to contain zero fabricated reviews, stars, or review counts.
4. **Private Route Secrecy:** Search engines are explicitly forbidden via `/robots.txt` and `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` from crawling or indexing private `/room/*` or `/history/*` routes.
